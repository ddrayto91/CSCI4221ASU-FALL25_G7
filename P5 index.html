Kitchen Math Cheat Sheet

  • 3 tsp = 1 tbsp
  • 16 tbsp = 1 cup
  • 1 cup flour ≈ 125g
  • 1 cup liquid ≈ 240ml
Pro Tip: When scaling up 4x or more, reduce salt and spices by 10% then taste-test!
